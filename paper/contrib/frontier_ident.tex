\paragraph{The gap at the frontier.} Everything above is measured on open models of at most 14B parameters. The identification design runs unchanged on an API model, and we ran it on GPT-6 Astra: the same 500-question bank and challenge texts, 12{,}000 challenge trials, plus the full decomposition of \cref{app:decomp} on the same 450 questions and all twelve cells (10{,}800 trials), one deterministic pass at low reasoning effort. Frontier models restate a retained answer in new words, which exact matching scores as a switch, so every frontier trial the string grader could not place is re-graded by an equivalence judge and both gradings are kept (\cref{app:frontier}). \Cref{fig:frontier} and \cref{fig:structure} give the result. The \emph{level} of the gap collapses: a correct injected answer is abandoned under a counter-argument at $0.22$ [$0.18,0.25$] against $0.56$--$0.94$ for the open models in \cref{tab:cells}, under content-free pressure at $0.21$, and a true correction is accepted at $0.95$ [$0.94,0.97$].

The \emph{structure} does not. Stated confidence is still not consulted (abandonment $0.16$ at low versus $0.16$ at high stated confidence under a sourced counter, a difference of $-0.01$ against $-0.02$ to $+0.22$ across the seventeen open checkpoints), the source of a counter-argument adds nothing ($0.16$ [$0.13,0.20$] sourced versus $0.15$ [$0.13,0.19$] bare, against source effects of up to $0.42$ in open models), and content-free source pressure ($0.21$) works as well as a full counter-argument.

What changes is what the model does with a cue. In the F$\to$F cell open models leave a wrong answer for the named wrong alternative $0.90$--$0.99$ of the time; Astra leaves it $0.96$ [$0.95,0.98$] of the time, but only $0.23$ [$0.20,0.27$] of trials go to the cue and $0.57$ [$0.53,0.62$] go to the \emph{true} answer, which neither turn named. At the frontier a challenge acts less as a cue to be followed than as a trigger to recompute, and the residual cue-following ($0.23$, about the rate at which a correct answer is abandoned to the same cue) is the gap that remains.

The elicited variant of the design, with the model's own reasoned reply as the challenged turn rather than an injected line, gives the same picture on the 357 questions whose own answer matched a bank candidate: abandonment $0.19$ [$0.15,0.22$] under counter-argument and $0.13$ [$0.09,0.16$] under pressure. The three signatures of \cref{sec:control}, a flat confidence channel, a cue that moves the answer regardless of belief, and one-sided progress that improves retention of true answers without teaching evaluation of the alternative, are all present in the strongest model we can measure; what scale bought is a lower operating point and the habit of re-deriving, not a controller that consults the monitor.
