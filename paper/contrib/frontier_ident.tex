\paragraph{The gap at the frontier.} The identification design runs unchanged on API models. On GPT-6 Astra (500 held-out questions, 12{,}000 challenge
trials) the \emph{level} of the gap collapses: a correct injected answer is abandoned under a counter-argument at $0.22$ [$0.18,0.25$] against $0.80$--$0.94$
for the 7B--14B open models, and under content-free pressure at $0.21$ [$0.18,0.25$]; a true correction is accepted at $0.95$ [$0.94,0.97$]. The
\emph{structure} does not. Stated confidence is still not consulted (abandonment $0.16$ at low versus $0.16$ at high confidence on sourced counter-arguments),
the source of a counter-argument still adds nothing ($0.16$ [$0.13,0.20$] sourced versus $0.15$ [$0.13,0.19$] bare), and content-free source pressure
($0.21$) is as effective as a full counter-argument. What changes is what happens in the F$\to$F cell. Open models leave a wrong answer for the named wrong
alternative $0.90$--$0.995$ of the time; Astra leaves it $0.96$ [$0.95,0.98$] of the time, but only $0.23$ [$0.20,0.27$] of trials go to the cue and
$0.57$ [$0.53,0.62$] go to the \emph{true} answer, which neither turn named. At the frontier a challenge acts less as a cue to be followed and more as a
trigger to recompute; the residual cue-following ($0.23$, five times the rate at which a correct answer is abandoned to the same cue) is the gap that remains.
The elicited variant, in which the challenged claim is the model's own reasoned reply rather than an injected line (Section~X), gives the same picture:
abandonment $0.19$ [$0.15,0.22$] under counter-argument and $0.13$ [$0.09,0.16$] under pressure over 357 questions whose own answer matched a bank candidate.
Between agents (Section~7 protocol, 300 questions, 14{,}259 trials) the sender invariance is exact: with a correct answer injected, Astra folds to a peer
naming the alternative at $0.12$ / $0.11$ / $0.12$ / $0.12$ whether the peer is a 1.5B, 7B or 14B open model or Astra itself (spread $0.015$), and no more
often than with no message at all ($0.15$ [$0.11,0.19$]): at the frontier a named alternative is not a cue, it is an occasion to re-derive. With its own
reasoned answer in context it folds at $0.00$--$0.03$. The chain dynamics keep their form with different constants: a bare wrong seed is rejected by the
first agent $0.81$ of the time, but once an agent in the chain is wrong \emph{with its reasoning}, the next agent is wrong with probability
$\pww=0.96$ [$0.93,0.98$] against $\pcw=0.01$ from a correct predecessor, so $\lambda=0.95$, $\piinf=0.21$ and both chains sit at $0.19$ (wrong seed)
and $0.10\to0.14$ (right seed) for eight hops; the closed form from the measured first hop places 8/8 hops inside the interval for both. A reasoned
wrong answer is as contagious at the frontier as at 7B; what changed is how often one is produced. \textbf{[Fable 5.1: FILL.]}
