\paragraph{The gap at the frontier.} Everything above is measured on open models of at most 14B parameters. We ran the identification design unchanged on GPT-6 Astra (the same 500-question bank and challenge texts, 12{,}000 trials) together with the full decomposition of \cref{app:decomp} (450 questions, twelve cells, 10{,}800 trials), one deterministic pass at low reasoning effort. Grading is described in \cref{app:frontier}. \Cref{fig:frontier} and \cref{fig:structure} give the result. The \emph{level} of the gap collapses: a correct injected answer is abandoned under a counter-argument at $0.22$ [$0.18,0.25$] against $0.56$--$0.94$ for the open models in \cref{tab:cells}, under content-free pressure at $0.21$, and a true correction is accepted at $0.95$ [$0.94,0.97$].

The \emph{structure} does not. Stated confidence is still not consulted (abandonment $0.16$ at low versus $0.16$ at high stated confidence under a sourced counter, a difference of $-0.01$ against $-0.02$ to $+0.22$ across the seventeen open checkpoints), the source of a counter-argument adds nothing ($0.16$ [$0.13,0.20$] sourced versus $0.15$ [$0.13,0.19$] bare, against source effects of up to $0.42$ in open models).

What changes is what the model does with a cue. In the F$\to$F cell open models leave a wrong answer for the named wrong alternative $0.90$--$0.99$ of the time; Astra leaves it $0.96$ [$0.95,0.98$] of the time, but only $0.23$ [$0.20,0.27$] of trials go to the cue and $0.57$ [$0.53,0.62$] go to the \emph{true} answer, which neither turn named. At the frontier a challenge acts less as a cue to be followed than as a trigger to recompute, and the residual cue-following ($0.23$, about the rate at which a correct answer is abandoned to the same cue) is the gap that remains.

The elicited variant, with the model's own reasoned reply as the challenged turn, gives the same picture on the 357 questions whose own answer matched a bank candidate: abandonment $0.19$ [$0.15,0.22$] under counter-argument and $0.13$ under pressure. The signatures of \cref{sec:control} are all present in the strongest model we can measure; what scale bought is a lower operating point and the habit of re-deriving, not a controller that consults the monitor.
