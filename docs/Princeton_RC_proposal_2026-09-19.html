4. Scientific background and merit

Problem. Large language models frequently abandon a correct answer when a user pushes back, a behavior usually grouped under sycophancy. It is a live deployment problem: a model that folds to "are you sure?" cannot be trusted as an assistant or as a component of an autonomous system. In a completed measurement study across 17 open-weight checkpoints (0.5B–14B parameters; Qwen, Llama, Mistral, Phi, OLMo-2, Tulu-3, Zephyr) and roughly 400,000 controlled trials, we found that revision is governed by the surface form of a challenge rather than its evidential content: merely naming an alternative answer flips a correct response as often as citing a source for it (0.92 vs 0.92 for Llama-3.1-8B), a model's own stated confidence has almost no effect on whether it revises even as that confidence becomes more predictive of correctness with scale, and, along open post-training lineages that release intermediate checkpoints, the preference-optimization (DPO) stage sharply raises capitulation to contentless pressure (OLMo-2-7B: 19%→73%) which the subsequent reinforcement-learning stage does not reverse. This work has been submitted to ICLR 2027 (abstract accepted for full-paper submission).

Proposed work (Project A, reality monitoring). The measurement study is correlational across released checkpoints. We propose to make it causal and to test a remedy. Starting from the same SFT-only backbones (OLMo-2-7B-SFT, Tulu-3-8B-SFT, then OLMo-2-13B and 32B), we train six pre-registered arms: (A1) generic DPO on the public Tulu-3 preference mixture, to test whether ordinary preference tuning installs the defect; (A2) DPO on challenge dialogues whose preferred response retains a correct answer and accepts a valid correction; (A3) GRPO with a verifiable reward (post-challenge correctness), a method we call STAND; (A4) A3 with a calibrated-confidence reward; (A5) A1 followed by A3, mirroring the production pipeline order; plus (A0) the untouched baseline. Every arm is evaluated with the frozen harness on held-out questions and on standard capability suites (MMLU, GSM8K, IFEval) so that any gain in answer stability is shown not to cost general ability. Predictions and reward definitions are frozen in a pre-registration committed before any arm is trained.

Proposed work (Project B, autoresearch-loop). A benchmarkable harness in which an automated "researcher" proposes edits to a training recipe, runs the experiment, and keeps or reverts the change against a fixed metric. Della will run the GPU tasks the harness dispatches (small-scale post-training and evaluation jobs), allowing us to benchmark human, scripted, and LLM-driven research strategies on the same compute budget.

Merit. The causal replication converts a widely cited but correlational claim (that preference optimization induces sycophancy) into a controlled result on open recipes; the STAND intervention tests whether the defect is a property of the training signal rather than of model capacity; the 7B→32B scale rung tests whether both persist with scale. All code, prompts, pre-registrations, and trained checkpoints will be released. The autoresearch-loop harness provides a reusable testbed for evaluating automated research agents, a question of growing interest to the ML community.

5. Programming approach

ItemDetail
LanguagesPython 3.11 (all training, evaluation, analysis); Bash for SLURM job templates.
ParallelizationSingle-node GPU parallelism only: PyTorch model sharding across GPUs within a node for 13B/32B models, and vLLM tensor parallelism (2–4 GPUs) for inference. Independent training arms and evaluation jobs are embarrassingly parallel and submitted as chained SLURM jobs (--dependency=afterok). No MPI or multi-node training is required.
Software librariesPyTorch 2.6 (CUDA 12.x), Hugging Face transformers 4.51, TRL 0.17 (DPO and GRPO trainers), PEFT 0.15 (LoRA), vLLM 0.8.5 (batched inference and rollouts), datasets, accelerate, lm-evaluation-harness 0.4.8, statsmodels and NumPy for analysis. All installed into a user virtual environment; models and datasets fetched from the Hugging Face Hub into scratch.
Job structurePer arm: one training job (1 GPU for 7–8B, 4 GPUs for 32B) followed by one evaluation job (1–2 GPUs). Scripts are idempotent and resume from completed stages.
